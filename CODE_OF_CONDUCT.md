# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and scientifically constructive
project. Participants must communicate professionally, welcome questions,
credit contributions, respect privacy, and critique ideas or code rather than
people. Harassment, discriminatory language, intimidation, and the disclosure
of private information are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns
privately to robin@kohze.com. Reports will be handled as confidentially and
promptly as practicable.
